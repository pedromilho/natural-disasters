# Natural Disasters

A natural disaster mod for Hytale.

Use `/naturaldisasters` to open the in-game test panel and start or stop a disaster.

## Blizzard

- Light snow starts the 10-second warning. The wind picks up at 5 seconds, and white fog rolls in as the blizzard begins.
- The storm lasts 60 seconds and covers an area with a radius of 128 blocks.
- The wind blows snow in a randomly chosen direction and pushes exposed players in Adventure mode.
- The fog is visible from outside the storm.
- Frost creeps inward from the edges of the screen. After 24 seconds of exposure, the cold deals 10 damage every 4 seconds.
- Shelter protects against the wind and cold. Lit campfires help you warm up.

## Earthquake

- A three-second warning precedes the shaking. Light, moderate, and strong earthquakes have radii of 72, 108, and 144 blocks.
- Each intensity has a distinct rumble. The camera shakes and players are pushed in changing directions, with stronger effects near the epicenter.
- Fissures start at the surface and favor ground above caves. Length, width, and depth vary, with extreme sizes occurring less often.
- Light and moderate earthquakes have a 12% and 40% chance of opening a fissure, respectively, when suitable ground is available.
- Strong earthquakes require enough exposed natural ground to open a large fissure. The ground splits as the shaking starts, with a loud rupture and a camera jolt.
